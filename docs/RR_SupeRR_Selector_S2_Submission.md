## Why Pressure Analytics?

Standard batting averages and bowling economies tell you how a player performs on average. **Pressure analytics tells you how they perform when it matters most** — when RR needs 15+ runs per over with half the batting gone.

```
Composite Score = 35% Pressure SR/Economy  +  25% Boundary/Dot Ball%
               + 20% Pressure Average/Wickets  +  15% Dismissal/SR Control
               +  5% Six-hitting/SR (inverted for bowlers)
```

All scores are **percentile-ranked within the candidate pool** — purely data-driven, no subjective weighting.

## 🔬 Methodology — How Rankings Were Built

```
STEP 1: Data Preparation
  200,999 ball-by-ball deliveries loaded
  Joined with match metadata → split into T20 (IPL) + T20I (International)
  Recency weights applied: 2025-26 = 2×, 2024 = 1.5×, 2022-23 = 1×

STEP 2: Pressure Delivery Identification
  Filter: 2nd innings only
  Compute RRR = (runs required) / (balls remaining) × 6
  Pressure ball = RRR ≥ 15 AND wickets fallen ≥ 3

STEP 3: Player Scoring (within pressure deliveries only)
  Batters:  P-Strike Rate, P-Average, Boundary%, Six%, Dismissal Rate
  Bowlers:  P-Economy, Dot Ball%, Boundary Conceded%, Wickets, Bowling SR

STEP 4: Composite Scoring
  Each metric percentile-ranked within the candidate pool
  Batters:  35% SR + 25% Boundary + 20% Average + 15% Dismissal(inv) + 5% Six
  Bowlers:  35% Economy(inv) + 25% Dots + 20% Boundary(inv) + 15% Wickets + 5% SR(inv)

STEP 5: RR Fit + Combined Score
  RR Fit: Auction Available = 100 | RR Retained = 85 | Other Retained = 0
  Combined = 70% × Composite + 30% × RR Fit
  
STEP 6: Availability Verification
  All 10 franchise retention lists cross-checked (official IPL auction graphics)
  Confirmed ineligible removed: Nitish Reddy (SRH), Sai Kishore (GT), Vyshak (PBKS)
```
